## About / Why "Tentacle"

### Built like the animal, not just named after it

An octopus doesn't manage one problem at a time. Each arm thinks, senses, and reacts on its own — nine brains working as one creature, never losing track of what the others are doing.

That's the whole idea behind Tentacle. Instead of one inbox, one backlog, one person trying to hold the entire organization in their head, Tentacle lets every request move independently — routed to the right category, the right position, the right person — while the system keeps a single, unified view of everything in motion.

No task is ever really separate. It's just another arm doing its job.

---

## How It Works

### Everything is a Request. Everything gets a Response.

1. **Raise it.** Anyone submits a Request — a bug, a task, an approval, a question. It's categorized the moment it lands.
2. **Route it.** Tentacle matches the category to the responsible position automatically. No more "who do I even send this to?"
3. **Work it.** The right person picks it up, moves it through status — Open, In Progress, Done — and everyone downstream sees it happen in real time.
4. **Respond it.** Every Request closes with a Response. Nothing just disappears. Nothing goes unanswered.

## Features

**Smart Routing**
Categories aren't just labels — they decide who's responsible. Set it once, and every future request finds its way home on its own.

**One Pane, Every Team**
HR, operations, engineering, whatever you run — Tentacle doesn't care what department raised the request. It only cares that it gets answered.

**A Dashboard That Actually Tells You Something**
Open vs. resolved, by category, by team, by person. See where work is piling up before it becomes a problem.

**Built to Extend**
New request type? New category? New team? Tentacle's structure was designed to grow with you, not around you.

**Full Audit Trail**
Every request, every response, every status change — timestamped and traceable. Nothing is ever "he said, she said."
